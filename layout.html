Welcome to Nuraqua Facilities

We pride ourselves on providing a top-tier environment for swimmers of all levels. Our academy features state-of-the-art pools, comfortable locker rooms, and dedicated lanes for both professional athletes and beginners.

Why Choose Our Academy?

  • Olympic Standard Pools: 50-meter and 25-meter lanes with perfect temperature control (27-28°C).
  • Safety First: Certified lifeguards on duty at all times and a multi-stage UV water purification system.
  • Professional Equipment: Kickboards, pull buoys, fins, and resistance bands available for all students.
